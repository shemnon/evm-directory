consensus is not a weaker answer than validity or fault — it is the honest one for a chain whose own validator set is the only attestation there is, and the axis names it rather than leaving the cell blank. proving_live exists so a roadmap cannot read as a fact: a proof system in a repository, on a testnet, or behind a whitelisted prover is a different claim from one adjudicating mainnet today, and halted is a fourth thing again. prover_constraints: lifted is deliberately kept rather than deleted — Scroll's 32-byte modexp cap ended at Galileo, and every contract deployed under it is still deployed. On the second half, the three ways to fail canonical are not one failure: deferred needs only an offset, foreign-hash needs the other hash function, and no-commitment has no workaround at all.
