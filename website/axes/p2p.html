Every size carries the tier that enforces it. consensus means a block breaking it is invalid; policy means only the mempool objects, so an oversized transaction is still perfectly valid in a block and merely cannot get there by gossip; transport means the wire will not move it. Mainnet's 128 KiB is policy — it is not a rule, and the gap between it and the ~1.6 MB that EIP-7825 and EIP-7623 jointly permit is the space private orderflow occupies.
