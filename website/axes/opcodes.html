The same chain placed batchValidateSign on BLAKE2F's 0x09 and made 0x03 return a value that is not RIPEMD160. BSC, the most mainnet-faithful EVM here, placed its custom precompiles at 0x64–0x69, leaving 0x12–0x63 as headroom before mainnet reaches them.

0x45Kaia⚠️GASLIMIT
There is no gasLimit field in the Kaia header. The value seen by bytecode, and the value eth_getBlockByNumber reports, is the CONSTANT params.UpperGasLimit = 500000000 (0x1dcd6500) — a compile-time number synthesised for Ethereum compatibility, not a consensus quantity and not a limit on anything.
